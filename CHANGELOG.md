# Basic Veinminer & Timer - 1.0 Release

### ***GENERIC***
- Initial release, available for Fabric 1.21.1, 1.21.11, 26.1.2, 26.2, 26.3
- Can work fully server-side, with optional client instalation for visual effects

### ***ADDED CONTENT***
- Veinminer
  - Sneak to veinmine
  - Veinmined ores are affected by the tool's enchantments
  - Vein won't fully mine if broken with an incorrect tool for drops
  - Block breaking will stop if your tool is at 1 durability left
  - On the client, the vein's edges will be visibly highlighted
- Timber (Tree Chop)
  - Sneak to chop down
  - Requires any item under `minecraft:axes` item tag to chop down
  - Chopped down logs are affected by the tool's enchantments
  - Block breaking will stop if your tool is at 1 durability left
  - On the client, the vein's edges will be visibly highlighted
